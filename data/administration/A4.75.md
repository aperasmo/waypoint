A4.75 Exception to health requirements instructions for people entering New Zealand for medical treatment
Temporary entry instructions allow for some visitors to enter New Zealand for medical treatment or medical consultation (see
V3.40
for specific criteria). Such applicants are exempted from the requirement to be of an acceptable standard of health for the purpose of the visa applied for under V3.40.
