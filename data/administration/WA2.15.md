WA2.15 Requirements for Standard Accreditation
In addition to meeting the requirements for all employers set out at
WA2.10
and
WA2.30
, employers applying for Standard Accreditation must have no more than 5 jobs associated to them (
WA3.35.1
) at any one time.
Effective 29/09/2025
