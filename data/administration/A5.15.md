A5.15 Summary of character requirements
Instructions in the remainder of chapter A5 describe:
applicants who must not be granted a visa under the Immigration Act 2009 (see
A5.20
), unless granted a special direction;
applicants who will not be granted a residence class or temporary entry class visa (see
A5.25
and
A5.45
respectively), unless granted a character waiver;
applicants who will not normally be granted a residence class or temporary entry class visa where they pose a risk to New Zealand’s international reputation (see
A5.30
), unless a visa is granted in accordance with
A5.30.1
; and
applicants whose applications for a residence class visa will usually be deferred (see
A5.35
).
Note:
With the exception of
A5.20
(which is a replication of a part of the Immigration Act 2009), the instructions in the remainder of chapter A5 constitute immigration instructions as described in section 22 of the Immigration Act 2009.
