# WD3.1 Determining a Post-Study work visa

Applicants may be granted a Post-Study work visa if:

1. they hold an eligible qualification, which is a New Zealand qualification studied for the minimum duration below:
   1. a qualification at level 8 to 10 on the NZQF, studied full-time for at least 30 weeks in New Zealand; or
   2. a degree qualification at level 7 on the NZQF, studied full-time for at least 30 weeks in New Zealand; or
   3. a qualification at level 4 to 7 specified on the list of Qualifications Eligible for a Post-Study Work Visa (Appendix 13 – Green List), studied full-time in New Zealand for the full duration required to obtain it; and
2. they apply:
   1. no later than 3 months after the end date of their student visa for that qualification; or
   2. no later than 6 months after the end date of their student visa if the qualification is a Doctoral degree; or
   3. within 3 months of the end date of their student visa for the second qualification, if they completed a Bachelor's degree immediately followed by an Honours or Postgraduate Diploma and held consecutive student visas for these; or
   4. within 12 months of the end date of their student visa for the initial qualification, if they completed an eligible qualification immediately followed by a higher-level qualification that doesn't itself meet the requirements, holding consecutive student visas; and
3. they have:
   1. successfully completed the qualification for which the student visa was granted; or
   2. if a Doctoral student, provided evidence from their education provider that they have submitted their thesis for examination; and
4. they have written approval from MFAT (see U11.1(d)) if their studies were supported under the MFAT scholarship programme; and
5. they have NZ$5,000 in funds available to maintain themselves during their stay; and
6. they have not previously been granted a Post-Study work visa.

Note: Study weeks include exam preparation time, and exclude holidays, time repeating failed components, and time on a different qualification whose credits were transferred into the completed one.

## WD3.1.1 Evidential requirements for an acceptable non-degree qualification at levels 4 to 7

1. Where applicable, evidence that a non-degree qualification at level 4 to 7 meets the knowledge requirements on the Appendix 13 list includes:
   1. a declaration from the applicant confirming the course meets the knowledge requirements; or
   2. a letter from the education provider confirming this.
2. An immigration officer may request further evidence if not satisfied.
