# WD3.5 Currency and conditions of Post-study work visas

Applicants who meet WD3.1(a) to (f) are eligible for a Post-Study work visa with a duration set out below:

| Qualification completed | Full-time study duration in NZ | Visa duration granted |
|---|---|---|
| Non-degree level 7 qualification or below | Full duration required for the qualification | The duration the student spent studying it |
| Level 7 Bachelor's degree or level 8 qualification | At least 30 weeks | The duration the student spent studying it |
| Level 9 or 10 qualification (Master's or Doctoral) | At least 30 weeks | 3 years |
| Bachelor's Degree immediately followed by Honours or Postgraduate Diploma | At least 30 weeks per qualification | Total time spent studying both, max 3 years |
| Consecutive qualifications meeting WD3.1(b)(iv) | Initial qualification meets WD3.1(a) | Duration spent studying the initial qualification |
| Non-degree level 8 qualification or below requiring occupational registration, meeting WD3.5.5 | As per row 1 or 2 above | 1 extra year on top of study time, max 3 years total |

Notes:
1. Study duration is determined by the education provider (via offer of place or confirmation letter) and does not include extra time for repeating failed components.
2. An immigration officer may request further evidence if the actual study duration does not align with the expected length of study.
3. A visa granted on the basis of a Bachelor's degree or level 8–10 qualification allows work in any occupation, any location. Self-employment is permitted, but visa holders may not employ others.
4. A visa granted on a non-degree qualification at level 7 or below is restricted to the occupation specified in the Appendix 13 (Green List) entry for that qualification.

## WD3.5.5 Working towards occupational registration or affiliate membership

Applicants with a qualifying non-degree level 7-or-below qualification (or non-degree level 8) may get one additional year (max 3 years total) if:
1. they are working towards registration or affiliate membership with the Teaching Council of Aotearoa New Zealand or the New Zealand Institute of Quantity Surveyors; and
2. they need up to one additional year of work experience to obtain that registration.
