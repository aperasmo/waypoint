SR6.1 Care Workforce: Work to Residence objective
The objective of the Care Workforce: Work to Residence sub-category is to provide for the grant of resident visas to people who have worked in New Zealand for at least 24 months in a Care Workforce sector occupation listed in
Appendix 14.
