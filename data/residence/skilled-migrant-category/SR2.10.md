# SR2.10 English Language Requirements

## SR2.10.1 Minimum standard of English language for principal applicants

1. Principal applicants are considered to meet the minimum standard of English if they provide acceptable English language test results no more than two years old at the time the application is lodged (see SR2.10.5), obtained by sitting the test in person at a test centre. Tests taken remotely, including 'at home' tests, are not acceptable.
2. Other evidence that a principal applicant meets the minimum standard of English is:
   1. citizenship of Canada, the Republic of Ireland, the United Kingdom or the United States of America, provided the applicant has spent at least five years in work or education in one or more of those countries or Australia or New Zealand; or
   2. a qualification comparable to a New Zealand level 7 bachelor's degree gained in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the Republic of Ireland, the United Kingdom or the United States of America from at least two academic years of study in one or more of those countries; or
   3. a qualification comparable to a New Zealand qualification at level 8 or above, gained the same way, from at least one academic year of study.
3. An immigration officer may still require a test result even where other evidence is provided.
4. Applications must be declined if the principal applicant has not met the minimum standard of English.

## SR2.10.5 Acceptable English language test results for principal applicants

| Test | Minimum score required |
|---|---|
| IELTS (General or Academic) | Overall score of 6.5 or more |
| TOEFL iBT | Overall score of 79 or more |
| PTE Academic | Overall score of 58 or more |
| B2 First (FCE) / B2 First for Schools | Overall score of 176 or more |
| Occupational English Test (OET) | Grade C+ or higher in all four skills |

## SR2.10.10 English language requirements for non-principal applicants

1. Partners and dependent children aged 16 and older must either show they meet a minimum standard of English, or pre-purchase ESOL tuition (see SR2.10.25).
2. Non-principal applicants meet the minimum standard the same way as principal applicants, using the SR2.10.20 test table below, or equivalent citizenship/qualification evidence.

## SR2.10.20 Acceptable English language test results for non-principal applicants

| Test | Minimum score required |
|---|---|
| IELTS (General or Academic) | Overall score of 5.0 or more |
| TOEFL iBT | Overall score of 35 or more |
| PTE Academic | Overall score of 36 or more |
| B2 First (FCE) / B2 First for Schools | Overall score of 154 or more |
| Occupational English Test (OET) | Grade C or higher in all four skills |

## SR2.10.25 Pre-purchase of ESOL tuition

Non-principal applicants who do not meet the English standard may instead pre-purchase ESOL tuition from the Tertiary Education Commission (TEC), by paying the required charge to INZ, who collect it on TEC's behalf.

## SR2.10.30 Amount of ESOL tuition to be pre-purchased

The charge is tiered by how close the applicant's test score is to the pass mark (e.g. IELTS 4.5–4.9 costs less than IELTS below 3.5). If no test result is submitted when requested, the maximum charge of NZ$6,795 applies.

## SR2.10.55 Limited period to use ESOL tuition

Applicants must complete purchased ESOL tuition within five years of the date of payment, or forfeit it without refund.

## SR2.10.60 Refunds of ESOL tuition money

A refund may be granted on written request if the applicant and family did not take up residence, provided the request is made within six months of the expiry of travel conditions, and the applicant has not entered New Zealand as a resident. Only the ESOL entitlement portion is refundable, not INZ/TEC administration costs.
