SR3.15 Points Summary
The principal applicant may qualify for the following points from one skill category (income, qualification, or New Zealand registration):
| Income (SR3.25.1) | Points |
| --- | --- |
| At least 3 times the median wage (currently $105 per hour) | 6 |
| At least 2 times the median wage (currently $70.00 per hour) | 4 |
| At least 1.5 times the median wage (currently $52.50 per hour) | 3 |
or
| Qualification Equivalent under the New Zealand Qualifications Framework* (SR3.25.5) | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Level 10 Doctoral Degree | 6 |
| Level 9 Master’s Degree | 5 |
| Level 8 Bachelor Honours Degree or Postgraduate Diploma | 4 |
| Level 8 Postgraduate Certificate Level 7 Bachelor’s degree | 3 |
*If the principal applicant provides an International Qualifications Assessment that does not specify a listed qualification type, they may qualify for the points listed at
SR3.25.5(b)
.
or
| New Zealand registration, licensing, or certification (SR3.25.10, SR3.30) | Points |
| --- | --- |
| A registration, license, or certification must be listed at SR3.30, which defines the requirements and number of points awarded for each. | 6 |
| A registration, license, or certification must be listed at SR3.30, which defines the requirements and number of points awarded for each. | 5 |
| A registration, license, or certification must be listed at SR3.30, which defines the requirements and number of points awarded for each. | 4 |
| A registration, license, or certification must be listed at SR3.30, which defines the requirements and number of points awarded for each. | 3 |
In addition to one skill category, applicants may qualify for the following points for their skilled work experience in New Zealand:
| Skilled work experience in New Zealand (SR3.35) | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 3 years | 3 |
| 2 years | 2 |
| 1 year | 1 |
