WD2 Lodging an application under Post-Study work visa instructions
Applicants must:
lodge their Post-Study work visa application in the prescribed manner (see
E4.50
); and
provide evidence that they have completed a qualification in New Zealand that meets the requirements as set out at
WD3
; and
provide evidence that they have a minimum of $5,000 in funds available to maintain themselves during their stay in New Zealand.
An offer of employment is not required.
Effective 16/12/2025
