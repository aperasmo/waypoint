SR1.1 Overview
(a) This chapter contains instructions for the grant of the following resident visas:
    (i) Skilled Migrant Category (SR3);
    (ii) Green List: Straight to Residence (SR4);
    (iii) Green List: Work to Residence (SR5);
    (iv) Care Workforce: Work to Residence (SR6); and
    (v) Transport Sector Work to Residence visa (SR7).
(b) The Skilled Residence visas set out at (a)(ii) to (v) above are sub-categories of the Skilled Migrant Category.
(c) Applications for Green List: Straight to Residence and Green List: Work to Residence visas are prioritised in the order of processing above other skilled residence applications.
Effective 09/10/2023
