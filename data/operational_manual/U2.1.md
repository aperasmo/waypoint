U2.1 Persons requiring a student visa
See also Immigration Act 2009 ss 21, 22, 73, 74, 77
A person must obtain a student visa in order to undertake study or training in New Zealand, unless they are:
(a) a New Zealand citizen; or
(b) the holder of a residence class visa; or
(c) the holder of a student visa; or
(d) the holder of a limited visa granted for the express purpose of attending a programme of study or training; or
(e) the holder of any other type of temporary entry class visa whose conditions have been varied to authorise the holder to undertake a programme of study in New Zealand; or
(f) covered by the provisions of U2.5.
Effective 02/12/2013
