SR5.1 Objective
The objective of the Green List: Work to Residence sub-category is to provide for the grant of resident visas to people who have demonstrated they have skills to fill specified high skilled or hard to fill and high national importance roles in New Zealand by having worked in New Zealand for at least 24 months in a Tier 2 Green List occupation.
Effective 09/10/2023
