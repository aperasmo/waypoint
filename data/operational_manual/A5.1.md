A5.1 Visa applicants must meet character requirements
Applicants for all visas must:
(a) be of good character; and
(b) not pose a potential security risk.
If any person included in the application fails to meet the necessary character requirements and the character requirements are not waived (or a special direction is not granted, or the discretion available at A5.30.1(c) or (d) to grant a visa is not applied, as the case may be), the application may be declined.
Effective 25/09/2023
