U6.15 Graduation
(a) Students who have completed their studies and wish to remain after their current visa expires, to attend graduation ceremonies, must apply for either a visitor or a work visa.
(b) Immigration officers must be satisfied that applicants have completed their qualification.
Effective 29/11/2010
