U3.7 Confirmation of enrolment
Applicants who are returning to the same New Zealand education provider to continue a programme of study or training scheme may, instead of an offer of a place, provide a confirmation of enrolment from the education provider which shows:
(a) the name of the programme of study; and
(b) the annual fee for the programme of study; and
(c) whether the student is full-time (see U6.1.1) or part-time (see U7.20); and
(d) the dates and duration of all scheduled vacations if the programme of study or training scheme is one academic year or more.
Note:
For the information specified at U3.7(d), this can be provided in a separate document accompanying the Offer of Place or Confirmation of Enrolment.
Effective 02/12/2013
