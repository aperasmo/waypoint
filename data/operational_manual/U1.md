U1 Objectives
The purpose of New Zealand's student instructions is to contribute to New Zealand's sustainable economic development by:
(a) facilitating the entry of genuine students, with a focus on attracting and developing students who have the skills and talent New Zealand needs;
(b) increasing global connectedness;
(c) supporting the sustainable growth of export education capability;
(d) earning foreign exchange; and
(e) strengthening New Zealand education, while managing risk to New Zealand and maintaining social cohesion.
Effective 29/11/2010
