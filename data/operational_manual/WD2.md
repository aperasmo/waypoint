WD2 Lodging an application under Post-Study work visa instructions
(a) Applicants must:
    (i) lodge their Post-Study work visa application in the prescribed manner (see E4.50); and
    (ii) provide evidence that they have completed a qualification in New Zealand that meets the requirements as set out at WD3; and
    (iii) provide evidence that they have a minimum of $5,000 in funds available to maintain themselves during their stay in New Zealand.
(b) An offer of employment is not required.
Effective 16/12/2025
