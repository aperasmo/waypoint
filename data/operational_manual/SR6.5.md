SR6.5 Summary of requirements
(a) To be granted a Care Workforce: Work to Residence visa, all applicants must:
    (i) meet health and character requirements for residence (see A4 and A5); and
    (ii) meet the applicable minimum standard of English or, where instructions allow, pre-purchase English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL) tuition (see SR2.10).
(b) In addition, the principal applicant must:
    (i) hold a work visa or a critical purpose visitor visa with work conditions at the time the residence application is made; and
    (ii) meet acceptable employment requirements in a Care Workforce occupation (see Appendix 14); and
    (iii) have completed 24 months in a Care Workforce sector occupation listed in Appendix 14 (see SR6.10); and
    (iv) meet the relevant wage rate in effect at the time:
        - they began to accrue work experience in New Zealand that counts towards their residence requirements; or
        - their work visa was granted, if it was granted in the five months before the applicant began to accrue their work experience in New Zealand; and
    (v) be aged 55 or younger at the time the residence application is made.
(c) An applicant may be considered to meet the relevant wage rate when they apply for residence if they meet the rate for their occupation in effect on the applicable date specified in (b)(iv) above.
(d) For the purpose of these instructions, applicants must accrue 24 months of work experience in New Zealand within the 30 months immediately prior to submitting their residence application.
    Note: For the purposes of determining whether work experience has been completed within the 30 month period, this period is calculated backwards from the date the resident visa is submitted.
SR6.5.1 Evidence
(a) To demonstrate meeting specified evidential requirements for a Care Workforce occupation (see Appendix 14) the applicant can provide the following:
    (i) Acceptable employment in a specified occupation - employment agreement and job description specifying the occupation.
    (ii) Acceptable work in New Zealand - employment agreement and job description (previous and current) stating the duration of employment, position and remuneration and a summary of earnings from Inland Revenue confirming a period of at least 24 months’ work.
    (iii) Pay rate - employment agreement and job description stating the occupation, hours, and remuneration. A summary of earnings from Inland Revenue if the applicant has worked in New Zealand.
        Note: Where an immigration officer is not satisfied that sufficient information has been provided through the requirements under (a)(iii), further evidence may be requested such as full bank statements showing salary payment or payslips.
(b) In cases where any of the details listed at SR6.5.1(a) have changed since an applicant’s most recent work visa application and these details are material to their application (for example their remuneration has changed and they are relying on work in New Zealand in a role paid at least twice median wage (SR5.20), an applicant must include the evidence of these changes as set out in SR6.5.1(a).
(c) An immigration officer may consider evidence and information provided in previous work visa applications to determine whether the applicant:
    (i) has a total of 24 months of acceptable work in New Zealand; or
    (ii) was paid the relevant wage rate (SR6.15(l)) .
Effective 24/08/2026
