SR4.10 Acceptable employment in a Tier 1 Green List role
(a) Acceptable employment in a Tier 1 Green List occupation is employment where the immigration officer is satisfied that:
    (i) the principal applicant holds current employment or an offer of employment for an occupation listed in Tier 1 of Appendix 13; and
    (ii) the requirements of that occupation specified in Tier 1 of Appendix 13 are met; and
    (iii) the remuneration for the employment or offer of employment is either:
        - at or above the hourly rate specified in Tier 1 of Appendix 13 where an occupation has remuneration requirements; or
        - at or above the median wage of $35.00; and
    (iv) the employment or offer of employment is with an employer who is accredited (and their accreditation is not suspended) at the time the residence application is either made or decided; and
    (v) the employment is full-time (employment is full-time if it amounts to at least 30 guaranteed hours per week for every week worked); and
    (vi) the employment is permanent or for a fixed term of at least 12 months from when the application is made; and
    (vii) the employment is genuine and is not offered as a result of payment (see SR2.1.5).
(b) For the purpose of the acceptable employment instructions, a contract for services will be considered acceptable employment:
    (i) where the applicant has a current contract or contracts for services totalling at least 6 months duration; and
    (ii) where a wage threshold and relevant work experience is specified for an occupation in Tier 1 of Appendix 13, the applicant meets the requirements specified for contract for service arrangements for that occupation.
(c) For independent midwife practitioners, a letter of authority to claim under the Maternity Notice pursuant to section 94 of the Pae Ora (Healthy Futures) Act 2022 and evidence of registration with the Midwifery Council is sufficient to meet the requirements at (b)(i).
(d) For roles that do not require occupational registration, an immigration officer may be required to assess whether the employment matches the Green List occupation. In determining whether employment is for a role on the Green List an immigration officer may undertake a substantial match assessment (see SR2.1.1).
Notes
:
- The median wage of $35.00 for Green List Tier 1 reflects the June 2025 publication of median wage data by Statistics New Zealand.
- For the purposes of undertaking a substantial match assessment, immigration officers must make an assessment based on Version 1.2 of the ANZSCO is available at
www.immigration.govt.nz/anzsco
.
